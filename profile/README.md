# Human–AI Mathematics

What a framework for mathematics done with AI has to do, what already exists, and a modest
proposal for one part of it.

**[human-ai-mathematics.github.io](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/)**

AI systems now produce mathematics in quantity, and the community has answered with many
projects, each addressing one part of the problem. What is missing is a view of the whole:
what a framework has to do, which parts are well served, and where nothing exists yet.

## Survey

### [The landscape](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/landscape/survey.html)

What a framework for mathematics with AI has to do, and about 150 projects mapped against it,
layer by layer, from sources to communication (survey dated 21 October 2026).
[Three architectures](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/landscape/architectures.html)
compares ways of connecting what exists: an integrated platform, a federation, or a registry
for research records.

## Proposal

### [Conjecture search](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/conjecture-search/proposal.html)

A shared, certified record of a sustained human–AI search on one hard statement, in which
every status is certified by an independent review or a named person.
[How it works](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/conjecture-search/how-it-works.html):
the life of a statement, the repository, and how to contribute.

- **[The KLS theorem and its methods](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/kls-conjecture-search/)**
  — our testbed. KLS was proved three times in October 2026, while our search was running; the
  manuscript became an account of the three proofs, their comparison, and alternative
  mechanisms for the dimension-free Poincaré bound.
- **[conjecture-search-template](https://github.com/human-ai-mathematics/conjecture-search-template)**
  — the reusable research harness behind it: a MyST manuscript, a ledger of certified claims,
  and researcher, reviewer and writer agents. It can start a search on any statement.

## Feedback

The site is meant to be temporary: this work makes most sense as part of a larger initiative,
and the site will point there when one exists. Until then, start a
[discussion](https://github.com/orgs/human-ai-mathematics/discussions) or open an
[issue](https://github.com/human-ai-mathematics/human-ai-mathematics.github.io/issues), or
write to nicolas.brosse [at] ensae.fr.
